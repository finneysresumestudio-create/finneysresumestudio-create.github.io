Resume Services Agreement

Finney's Resume Studio

Please review this agreement, then print and sign it (or sign your downloaded copy). Email the signed agreement to finneysresumestudio@gmail.com — a scan or clear phone photo is perfectly fine. A signed agreement is required before any work can proceed.

1Parties

This Agreement is between:

  • Service Provider: Finney's Resume Studio (Laura Finney)
  • Client:  

Both parties agree to the terms outlined below.

2Scope of Services

Finney's Resume Studio provides professional resume-related services. Depending on the package purchased, services may include:

  • Resume writing or rewriting
  • Resume template creation
  • Cover letter template
  • LinkedIn profile optimization

Career coaching is not included in any package.

3Client Requirements and Expectations

To ensure timely delivery and maintain quality standards, the Client agrees to the following:

  • The intake form must be completed in full before the delivery window begins.
  • The Client must remain reachable and responsive throughout the project.
  • Any clarifying questions from Finney's Resume Studio must be answered within 3 hours.
  • Delays beyond 3 hours may extend the delivery timeline by full calendar days, not hours.
  • Rush timelines apply only to the resume deliverable included in the purchased package.
  • The Client must be available for quick follow-up questions to maintain "rush" accelerated schedules.
  • All fees are non-refundable once work begins.

4Payment Terms

  • 100% payment is required up front.
  • No refunds are issued once work begins.
  • Payment covers only the deliverables included in the selected package.

5Engagement Window

  • All packages include a 30-day engagement window.
  • The Client must complete all required steps within this timeframe.
  • If the Client does not complete their steps, the project closes automatically with no refund.

This policy protects the Service Provider's time and ensures fairness to all clients.

Important: To maintain fairness and scheduling efficiency, all timelines must be met as outlined. If the Client's delays prevent progress within the agreed schedule, Finney's Resume Studio reserves the right to charge an additional fee of $20 per day for each day of delay. This fee must be paid in full prior to receiving the next draft.

If the Client requires additional time beyond the 30-day engagement window, the window may be extended once for an additional fee of $100, payable before any further work is completed. If the continuation fee or extension fee is not paid, Finney's Resume Studio may close the project without refund.

7Communication

  • All communication will occur via email.
  • Standard response time is 24 hours.
  • When actively working on a resume (drafting or revising), expected response time is 12 hours.
  • The Client must maintain timely communication to avoid timeline extensions.

8Confidentiality

Finney's Resume Studio will keep all client information confidential and will not share or distribute any personal or professional data.

9Satisfaction Clause

Finney's Resume Studio does not guarantee job placement, interview offers, or employment outcomes. Services focus on producing high-quality career documents.

10Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement with written notice. Work completed up to the termination date will be billed accordingly. No refunds are issued once the agreement is completed.

11Intellectual Property, Template Use, Branding, and Ownership

All resume templates, cover letter templates, and related materials provided by Finney's Resume Studio are for the Client's personal use only. These materials may not be shared, distributed, reproduced, or used to create derivative works for any other individual or organization. Unauthorized use of templates is strictly prohibited. All branding, formatting, and stylistic elements of Finney's Resume Studio — including logo, color palette, and document structure — remain the exclusive property of the Service Provider.
